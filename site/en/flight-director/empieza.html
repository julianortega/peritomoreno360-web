Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum term?

No. You pay month by month and cancel whenever you want, with no penalty. And if something doesn't fit, let's talk.

How much does it cost?

€75 a month per location, plus VAT, with everything included. No paid modules or extras.

Will you raise the price?

No. The price you sign up at is the price you'll always pay.

What if I have several locations?

Sign up with the first one and we'll help you add the rest. Each location costs the same and you see them all from the same account.

Do I need to buy any devices?

No. Flight Director works in the browser on the phone, tablet or computer you already have. If you want screens, printers or a POS, we'll recommend what works best.

How do I pay each month?

By card or Bizum from a payment link you receive each month, with its invoice.

Can I see it before paying?

Of course. Write to us at info@peritomoreno360.com and we'll give you a demo with your own dishes.
