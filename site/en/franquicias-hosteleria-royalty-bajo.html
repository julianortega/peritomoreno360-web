What a franchise royalty is

The royalty is the percentage of sales the franchisee regularly pays the brand in exchange for using it and receiving its support: training, manuals, product improvements, technology and ongoing help.

It may look like a detail, but in hospitality, where the final margin is usually tight, a few royalty points make the difference between a business that makes money and one that only makes work. That's why it pays to look not just at the percentage, but also at whether there are other fees (advertising, technology…) and whether the royalty can go up over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the royalty calculated on sales or on profit?

Is there an entry fee?

It depends on the brand and the format. We give you all the figures (entry fee, estimated investment and contract length) at the first meeting.

Can the royalty go up in the future?

No. The 4% from year two is fixed, with no reviews.
