Frequently asked questions

How much does Mi Vestuario cost?

€75 a month per club, plus VAT, with everything included: all the teams, coaches, players and families you need.

Is there a lock-in period?

No. You pay month by month and can cancel whenever you like.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Mi Vestuario works in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

What does each person in the club see?

Everyone sees what matters to them: the board sees the whole club, each coach their team, and players and families their matches, notices, fees and permissions.

Can fees be collected through the app?

Yes. Families pay the fee by card and the club can always see what is outstanding.

What support do I get?

Very friendly support: a person from the team helps you by WhatsApp, phone or email during your club's activity hours, including match weekends. It's included in the price.

Can I see it before signing up?

Of course. Leave us your details and we'll show you Mi Vestuario with your club's teams.
