Business area 02

Operations: your company, more efficient.

We've spent years getting the operations of our own locations in order. Now we apply that method to your company: we study it in depth, find where time and money are being lost, and fix it with you.

What we do

01

An in-depth study of your company

We analyse processes, teams, costs and ways of working to know exactly how things run today and how they could run better.

  • Process and timing diagnosis
  • Review of costs and staffing
  • Report with clear priorities
02

Operations manuals

We write down how everything is done, so the business runs just as well whoever is on shift.

  • Step-by-step procedures
  • Recipe specs and checklists
  • Training material for the team
03

Weak spots, found and fixed

We look for where operations break down and don't stop at the report: we propose the solution and put it in place.

  • Bottleneck detection
  • Action plan with owners
  • Results tracking
04

Your operations assistant, every month

We act as a part-time operations manager: we review KPIs, support the team and solve whatever comes up.

  • Regular visits and meetings
  • Monthly KPI review
  • Direct support for management
