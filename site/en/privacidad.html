At Perito Moreno 360 SL we process your personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD). Here we explain how we do it.

1. Data controller

2. What data we process

Only what you give us in the contact form: name, email address, phone number and the area you're interested in. We also record the page you write to us from and the date it was sent.

3. What we use it for

To get in touch with you, by phone or email, and respond to your request for information about our services, brands or franchises. We don't use it to send you advertising unless you ask us to, and we don't make automated decisions with it.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and taking pre-contractual steps at your request (article 6.1.a and 6.1.b GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to deal with your request. If we don't go on to start a business relationship, we will delete it no later than 12 months after the last contact. If a relationship begins, it will be kept for its duration and then for the applicable legal periods.

7. Your rights

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. The website always uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
