Cookie policy

This is an English translation for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website does not use cookies: neither our own nor third-party ones, nor analytics, advertising or social media cookies. That's why we don't show you a cookie notice when you arrive.

To display its typefaces, the page loads files from Google Fonts. This service does not set cookies, but it receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. You can find more information in our privacy policy.

3. Changes

If we add cookies in the future, for example for analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

4. How to manage cookies in your browser

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge), under privacy.
