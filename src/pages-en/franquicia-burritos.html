Why a burrito franchise

Mexican fast casual combines three things customers look for today: food that looks fresh, fast service and an affordable bill. It also travels very well for delivery, so the same restaurant sells in-house and on delivery platforms at the same time.

For the franchisee, that means a short menu, processes that are easy to teach and a kitchen that can run with a small team.

Who is behind it

Bestial is part of the Perito Moreno 360 group. Since 2014 the founding team has been leading operations, people and openings at Mexican fast casual chains in Spain. Bestial brings all that experience together in a leaner, more natural model, with franchise terms designed so the franchisee wins first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a Bestial franchise?

It depends on the format and the premises. The dark kitchen is the lowest-investment option; the restaurant and flagship need more building work and equipment. We give you the full breakdown, with the entry fee and estimated investment, at the first meeting.

What royalty do I pay?

2% of sales in the first year and 4% from the second year, with no later increases.

Do I need hospitality experience?

It's not essential. We train you before opening, give you the operations manual and, if you prefer, we can take care of the day-to-day with our full management service.

Can I start with delivery only?

Yes. The dark kitchen is designed precisely as the entry model: you invest less, test demand in your area and, once it works, make the jump to a restaurant.

Which cities are you looking for franchisees in?

We want to take the brand all over Spain. Tell us where you'd like to open and we'll look at the location together.
