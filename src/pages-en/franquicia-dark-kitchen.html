Dark kitchen franchise

Dark kitchen franchise: start with a small investment and test your area.

A dark kitchen is a kitchen that only sells for delivery, through the delivery platforms. No dining room, no waiting staff, and small premises. It's the most cautious way to open a hospitality franchise: you invest less and check real demand before making the jump to a street-front restaurant.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dark kitchen franchise?

It's a restaurant franchise that only sells for delivery, through platforms such as Glovo, Uber Eats or Just Eat. It has no dining room, so it can be set up in small premises on cheaper streets.

How much space do I need?

For the Bestial dark kitchen format, between 15 and 50 m², with smoke extraction and a location suited to deliveries.

Can I move to a restaurant with a dining room later?

Yes. That's exactly how we see it: the dark kitchen tests the area and, when demand confirms it, you open a 50 to 100 m² restaurant with dine-in and delivery.

What is the royalty?

2% of sales in the first year and 4% from the second, with no increases.
